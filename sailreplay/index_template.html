Racing days

    Add your race

    1. After sailing, open Vakaros Connect, pick the session, and export it as a .vkx file.
    2. Keep the name it gives you, like Your Name_s_Vakaros_3-22-2026.vkx, or rename it after your boat, like Mojo.vkx. That name is what shows on the replay.
    3. Upload it with the button below: drag the file in, then press Commit changes.
    4. The site rebuilds itself. Your boat appears on that day's replay within about two minutes, raced against everyone else who sailed.
    Upload .vkx files

    You need a free GitHub account. If you are not on the fleet's list yet, GitHub turns your upload into a request, and it goes live once it is approved.

    Ping both ends of the start line on your Atlas and use its race timer. That turns on start-line analysis for the whole fleet.
