Scores

Preliminary

How these are scored. CYC Frostbite high point (Sailing Instructions 13): a boat scores one point for each boat she beats, plus one. A boat that was out but did not finish, or finished more than ten minutes after the first boat (SI 11.2), is DNF and scores 1. Not sailing a race (DNC), or being disqualified, scores 0. There are no throw-outs in a series. A regatta drops each boat's worst race when five or more are sailed, shown in brackets. A boat qualifies for a series prize by sailing on two or more days.

On a Preliminary day, places come from the order boats crossed the finish line in their uploaded Atlas logs, and only boats that uploaded are counted, so a race scores as if the fleet were smaller. Race committee credit, solo-sailor penalties, OCS and protests are not known from the logs. The official results, emailed and posted weekly on the CYC website, always take priority.
