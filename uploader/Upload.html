Upload your race

Pick your boat and your .vkx file from Vakaros Connect, or a GPS watch's .fit / .zip (one per race is fine, pick them all at once), or a phone recording (see below). It shows up on the site in about 3 minutes.

    Easier from your phone: in Vakaros Connect open the session, tap Share, choose Mail and send it to . Put your sail number in the subject.

    No Vakaros or watch? Try your phone. The free Sensor Logger app (iPhone and Android) records GPS and how the boat heels. We are collecting the first recordings to add them to the site, so send yours:

    1. In Sensor Logger turn on Location, Orientation and Compass (the rest can stay off).
    2. Put the phone in a waterproof case and strap it flat to the deck, screen up, along the centreline, not in a pocket.
    3. At the dock with the boat level, zero the orientation, then start recording before the first warning. One recording for the whole day.
    4. Afterwards export it as zipped CSV and upload the zip here, or email it with your sail number in the subject.

    GPS watch (Garmin): in Garmin Connect on a computer, open each race's activity, click the gear icon and choose Export File. Upload the files it saves here. For the best track, set the watch's Data Recording to Every Second.
